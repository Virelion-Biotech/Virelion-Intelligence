# Research beats

1. **Cardiac regeneration** — cardiomyocyte proliferation, reprogramming, stem/progenitor biology, scar remodeling and regenerative mechanisms.
2. **Ischemic heart** — myocardial infarction, ischemia/reperfusion, infarct/border/remote zones, inflammation, fibrosis, metabolism and remodeling.
3. **Cardiomyocyte maturation** — iPSC-cardiomyocytes, metabolic switching, electrophysiology, sarcomere maturation and disease modeling.
4. **Regenerative engineering** — engineered myocardium, biomaterials, hydrogels, scaffolds, conductive materials, bioprinting and vascularization.
5. **Cardiac omics** — scRNA-seq, snRNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, proteomics, multi-omics and atlas resources.
6. **Computational cardiology** — ECG AI, imaging AI, digital twins, simulation, foundation models and ML biomarkers.
7. **Datasets/software** — public datasets, benchmarks, GitHub releases, reproducibility tooling and useful open-source analysis packages.
8. **Translation** — clinical trials, therapies, biotech programs, manufacturing, regulatory developments, patents and partnerships.

Search broadly, then verify against primary sources. A quiet beat should remain quiet; never pad a report with recycled claims.
